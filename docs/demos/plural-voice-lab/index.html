Why quadratic cost, in one paragraph

Flat one-person-one-vote systems can't distinguish mild preference from intense preference. Simple summed scoring lets a single participant dominate by pouring unlimited weight into one option. Quadratic pricing sits between the two: it lets people signal intensity while making it progressively expensive to dominate a single outcome, which is the mechanism RadicalxChange has piloted in participatory-budgeting settings.
