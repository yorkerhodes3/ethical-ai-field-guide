Dignity & Agency Audit

Independently portable: single self-contained HTML file. Data saved only in this browser's localStorage.

What this is: an interactive self-assessment checklist across the DAPPR lenses (Dignity, Agency, Plurality, Power, Reversibility), inspired by the human-rights floor and do-no-harm ideas in the "What Is Ethical AI" executive summary and by Magnifica Humanitas's concerns about dignity and concentrated power. What this is not: a legal audit, a compliance certification, or a substitute for qualified legal, human-rights, or regulatory review. Answer honestly for a system, team, or policy you know well. Sources: What Is Ethical AI? · Magnifica Humanitas.

How to use these results responsibly

Unchecked items are not "failures" — they're prompts for discussion. This tool has no scoring threshold for "pass" or "fail," because none of the anchor sources propose one; the value is in the conversation an incomplete checklist provokes, not the final number.
