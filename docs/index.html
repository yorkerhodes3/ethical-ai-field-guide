Executive thesis

The Human Choice

AI, Power & Dignity Field Guide

Nine anchor sources — spanning a public essay, an executive summary, an encyclical, a collaborative book and its public reading interface, a governance movement, and two scenario documents — converge on one question: as AI capability accelerates, who keeps the power to decide, and can that decision still be reversed? This guide reads them side by side through five lenses of our own synthesis — Dignity, Agency, Plurality, Power, Reversibility (DAPPR) — without ever treating a scenario or a recommendation as settled fact.
