Seven review options

Plurality + human favicon study

Five candidates compose existing assets: four preserve the exact CC0 Plurality focus-logo geometry, while the selected option adapts its lattice for favicon clarity. Two candidates are original art. Each row shows the real SVG at 16, 24, 32, 48, and 96 pixels.

Deployed default: Option 2, Open Arms. The outstretched Material human remains the clearest at 16 pixels. Its circle is removed, color moves onto the exposed squares, and the lattice is inset so corner rounding cannot clip it.
