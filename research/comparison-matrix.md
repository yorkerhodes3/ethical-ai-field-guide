# Cross-source comparison matrix

The matrix compares genres that make different kinds of claims. A short cell
cannot replace the individual source brief.

## At a glance

| Source | Genre / posture | Primary problem | Human agency | Main governor | Greatest contribution | Principal blind spot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gates | Public essay; forecast + recommendation | Rapid substitution, concentrated gains, misuse, childhood and relationship harms | Control over future, work, critical thought, human care | New national and international transition bodies; democratic process | Connects benefits, labor, tax base, Human Reserved, and institution building | Treats global slowdown as largely infeasible and leaves implementation criteria open |
| *What Is Ethical AI?* | Institutional ethical argument | Power without ethical architecture across the lifecycle | Rights, do no harm, participation, remedy | Accountable institutions across problem selection through retirement | Makes ethical status institutional rather than a model property | Participation and enforcing institutions need more concrete allocation of authority |
| *Magnifica Humanitas* | Encyclical; theological and social doctrine | Technocracy, dehumanization, private digital power, loss of truth, work, relationship, peace | Freedom, conscience, participation, education, responsibility, relationship | Layered institutions shaped by dignity, common good, subsidiarity and solidarity | Richest account of the human person and most specific moral limits on private power | Translation into plural secular institutions requires care and further design |
| *Plurality* | Open book, manifesto, design agenda | Atomization and centralized technocracy | Collaboration through overlapping affiliations and publics | Open protocols, plural institutions, public investment, adaptive governance | Positive vision and concrete tools for cooperation across difference | Identity/privacy, agenda power, infrastructure dependence, and aspirational evidence |
| RadicalxChange | Nonprofit, mechanism wiki, project portfolio | Monopoly, plutocracy, centralized decisions, polarization | Expressive voice, bargaining, stewardship, exit | Participatory mechanisms and distributed institutions | Turns democratic theory into testable mechanisms and projects | Mechanisms can be complex, advisory, identity-dependent, or captured by agenda setters |
| Plurality.net | Publishing interface | Public access to the Plurality argument | Read, choose a pathway, contribute | Project authors/community and repository | Makes a complex open book accessible in multiple formats | Softer interface hides some polemic, governance, revision, and unresolved risks |
| *AI 2027* | Predictive scenario | Rapid automated R&D, race pressure, misalignment, late oversight | Mostly reactive outside labs and state executives | Frontier labs, US executive, China; then AI or oversight committee | Concrete causal chain, uncertainty markers, correction history | Narrow actor model; vivid dates can overpower uncertainty; both endings concentrate power |
| *AI 2040* | Recommendation in scenario form | Extinction and winner-take-all power under a superintelligence race | Human control is the goal; public authorship is less specified | US-China deal, Consortium, national regulators, auditors, permit regime | Time, visibility, diffusion, reversibility, and scenario scrutiny | Technical fragility, great-power legitimacy, new governors, handoff paradox |
| Reading-group handout | Facilitation document | How to discuss Plan A critically and concretely | Small-group deliberation and recorded forecasts | Facilitator and participant groups | Excellent structure around cruxes, rival plans, failures, and calibration | Date placeholder; version drift; limited treatment of global legitimacy and Plan S |

## Key disagreements

| Disagreement | Position A | Position B | Consolidated treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nature of intelligence | Gates treats reliable cognition as substitutable performance | Magnifica §99 treats human and machine intelligence as different in kind | Measure task performance; never infer moral standing or legitimate authority from rank |
| Pace | Gates sees global slowdown as desirable but implausible | Plan A makes coordinated slowdown the core prescription | Build no-regrets institutions and invest in diplomacy/verification that can change feasibility |
| Openness | Plurality emphasizes open protocols and collaboration | Plan A acknowledges open research can help covert defectors | Use tiered access, reciprocal duties, independent scrutiny, security and privacy |
| Scale | Plan A centers US-China and frontier compute | Subsidiarity and Plurality distribute voice across levels and publics | Use a global floor, capable local control, and appeal between levels |
| Work settlement | Gates proposes Human Reserved and machine taxation | Plan A imagines permits and dividends under radical abundance | Separate income, ownership, bargaining, contribution, care, status and community |
| Handoff | Plan A imagines transfer to trusted superintelligence | Dignity and democratic agency imply non-delegable human authority | Require separate authorization for advice, execution, coercion, adjudication and rule change |

## Missing voices in the corpus

The source set should be expanded before becoming a canonical semester
bibliography:

- workers organizing around deployment and productivity;
- disability communities and assistive-technology users;
- children and educators as participants, not only subjects of concern;
- Indigenous data sovereignty and collective rights;
- low- and middle-income country governments outside a US-China frame;
- communities near datacenters, mines, energy and water infrastructure;
- data labelers, moderators, logistics workers and other hidden labor;
- critics of both frontier-risk and acceleration narratives;
- feminist, abolitionist and decolonial technology scholarship;
- small and medium enterprises, public-sector implementers and local
  governments;
- religious and moral traditions beyond the Catholic source.

These are not decorative additions. They may change the problem definition,
not merely add examples.
