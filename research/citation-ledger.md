## Primary anchors used most often

### P1 - Gates

- Opening and transition claim.
- "The transition to AI comes with three big risks."
- "AI could stunt our kids' development and replace human relationships."
- "The good things we do with AI could be very, very good."
- "The world needs a plan."
- "Build a new system for managing the transition."
- "Set aside some jobs for humans."
- "Human Reserved" is Gates's term for activities deliberately kept
  substantially human even when substitution is technically possible.
- "Rebalance how we tax labor and capital."
- Childhood section: direct citation and discussion of Jonathan Haidt's *The
  Anxious Generation*.

endorses P3 as a foundation for the work.

## Citation cautions

1. **Live pages change.** Include access date and, for *Plurality*, release
   revision where possible.
2. **Scenario dates are not facts.** Cite them as events within the scenario.
3. **Organization and book differ.** Attribute RadicalxChange terms to the
   organization unless they are verified in the book.
4. **The Google document has no stated author or event date.** Do not infer
   either.
5. **P2 has institutional authorship but no named individual author.** Do not
   infer an individual from the hosting account.
6. **Paragraph numbers are preferred for the encyclical.**
7. **Search summaries are discovery aids.** Cite underlying pages.
