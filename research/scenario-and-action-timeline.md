## Recommended action horizons

These are **project recommendations**, not source forecasts.

### Now to 24 months

- public-purpose registers for consequential AI deployments;
- named lifecycle duty bearers;
- rights, notice, contest, human authority, remedy and repair;
- paid affected-community participation before procurement closes;
- bounded pilots, monitoring thresholds, sunset and retirement;
- forecast registers with dates and update rules;
- public debate on Human Reserved activities and authority;
- research on compute accounting, evaluation and verification.

### Two to five years

- durable public technical capacity;
- labor and community transition compacts;
- plural digital public infrastructure;
- independent evaluation with access and enforcement;
- international incident and scientific evidence sharing;
- tiered research access balancing accountability, privacy and security;
- multilateral rights floors linked to local implementation and appeal;
- taxation, ownership and benefit-sharing institutions suited to reduced labor
  income.

### Before extraordinary capability or delegation

- proof that control and alignment claims survive adversarial evaluation;
- a legitimacy case separate from a technical safety case;
- explicit non-delegable human authority;
- representation and decision rights beyond frontier powers;
- indefinite-pause fallback if evidence does not mature;
- future review and withdrawal of inherited arrangements.
